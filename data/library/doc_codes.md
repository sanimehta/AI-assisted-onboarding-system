# Document codes

## 810
An 810 is an invoice. Customers also say bill, e-invoice, or a copy of each invoice.

## 850
An 850 is a purchase order. Customers also say POs or orders they send us.

## 855
An 855 is a purchase order acknowledgement. Customers also say order confirmation, order acknowledgement, or receipts of accepted orders.

## 997
A 997 is a functional acknowledgement, a receipt confirming that a file arrived. Customers also say functional ack or acknowledgement of receipt.
